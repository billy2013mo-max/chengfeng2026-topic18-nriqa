# Grading, not detection, caps the fallback

The fallback ensemble does not have an evidence-backed path to a reliable 53+ using engineering levers alone. The one measured, compliant gain left is a better decision rule, worth about **+0.25 to +0.5 points on all rows and +0.25 to +0.3 on first-batch rows** in nested out-of-fold (OOF) runs. Every other engineering lever is either measured null or a speculative 0 to +0.3. The 13.65 points the fallback loses out of fold (51.35 of 65) come mostly from **ordinal grading**: perfect grading of detected defects is worth +9.32, perfect detection only +3.54. Sibling frames of the same video agree on grade only 50–61 % of the time, so most of that grading headroom sits at or near the labelling-consistency floor. On first-batch-like data (52.11 OOF) the fallback therefore needs about +0.9 to reach 53. A realistic engineering-only stack (decoder plus at most one new orthogonal member) gives **+0.3 to +0.6**. Owner-side levers (unlabelled first-batch frames, a label audit, new 过度生成 / 虚化突兀 labels) could add another +0.1 to +0.8, but none of them is measured inside the ensemble. **A gain of +1.5 to +2.0 is not supported by any measured evidence.** One 654-image final reading has an SD of about 0.56, so a 53 could appear by luck (about 6 % at today's mean, about 14–24 % after the realistic gains), but it cannot be engineered with confidence.

Two definitions matter for reading the numbers below. The statement exists in two versions. Version (1) defines **Clean Score = max(0, 1 − P0 FPR / τ)**, with τ set by the organisers ([statement v1](../documents/【2026乘风挑战赛】命题18：晴羽观感体系·画质BadCase的无参检测模型%20(1).md)). Version (2) defines S_clean as the share of fully clean images predicted clean on all six dimensions, where any defect at any grade counts as an error ([statement v2](../documents/【2026乘风挑战赛】命题18：晴羽观感体系·画质BadCase的无参检测模型%20(2).md)). The project's scorer `doc2_strict_total` implements version (2). That makes it the **stricter proxy**: it penalises P2/P1 false alarms on clean images that version (1) ignores, and it can never be inflated by a loose τ. Scores under version (1) can only be equal or higher. Second, all "OOF" numbers are nested over 8 video-grouped folds: the decoder for fold f is fitted on the other seven folds. CIs are video-cluster bootstraps ([error_analysis.log](../logs/r55/explore4/error_analysis.log); [check.log](../logs/r55/explore4/decoding/check.log)). The shipped 4-start Decoder55 scores 51.28 / 52.12 on the canonical partition and 51.20 / 51.98 averaged over four partitions. The 51.35 / 52.11 reference is the single-start nested score. Partition noise is ±0.1–0.2.

## Five rare grade cells, not clean images, drain the points

Of the 13.65 lost points, **S_pos loses 9.51, S_p0 3.75 and clean only 0.39** ([error_analysis.log](../logs/r55/explore4/error_analysis.log)). Of the 513 clean images, 503 are predicted fully clean. Nine of the ten failures are P2-level, mostly 纹理损失, and only one is a P0 false alarm. The term is at 98 % of its ceiling. On the P0 side, macro recall is 0.843 and precision 0.762: the decoder deliberately trades precision for recall because recall carries half of S_p0's weight. By defect, a perfect prediction would be worth **过度生成 +4.31, 过锐 +3.02, 模糊 +2.75, 纹理损失 +2.43, 虚化突兀 +0.93, 花屏 +0.10**. These oracle gains interact (fixing one label changes others' FP/FN), so they are not a budget that can be summed.

These are one confusion: **99 gold-P1 frames called P0, the single costliest cell (+1.35 if fixed)**. Other costly cells follow:

The two 虚化突兀 detection cells together are worth +0.94: 64 false positives and 30 misses ([error_followup.log](../logs/r55/explore4/error_followup.log)). Because macro-F1 weighs rare and frequent labels equally, a corrected rare-grade 过度生成 / 过锐 cell is worth **1.3–1.9 points per 100 cells, against 0.15–0.5 for 纹理损失 / 模糊**. Of the 591 rows predicted fully clean, 88 are defective, mostly mild 纹理损失. Fixing all 88 would gain only +0.29.

**By release batch and source.** The first batch scores 52.11 and the supplement 51.05. The difference has CI [−0.01, +2.21] and is borderline significant. All 513 clean images are first-batch, so a supplement-only score gets a free 20 on clean. Compared like for like, the gap is about 1.5–1.9 points ([error_followup.log](../logs/r55/explore4/error_followup.log)). Lower label prevalence works *against* the first batch (−0.52 in S_pos). The +0.87 accuracy part of the gap comes from easier content: first-batch timestamp frames average 0.45 wrong dimensions per row against 1.43 in the supplement. P0 false-positive rates are about 3× higher in the supplement. Per-row error rates on defective images are statistically indistinguishable between batches (1.037 vs 1.071 wrong dimensions). The batch gap is case mix, not a model deficit. That is also why batch weighting and batch-specific decoders measure null (see the last section). By source tag, the older v7L3HiV2 pipeline frames are the most expensive per row (0.86 points per 100 rows, 1.51 wrong dimensions per row). All 170 of them are first-batch, and practice/final frames come largely from first-batch videos, so they are the one sensible hard-example target. They are a target for review, not for up-weighting.

**Label consistency caps the grading headroom.** When both frames of a video are gold-defective, they agree on the *grade* only **0.60 (模糊), 0.61 (纹理损失), 0.57 (过锐) and 0.50 (过度生成)**. The model's grade accuracy given detection is 0.70 / 0.74 / 0.71 / 0.52. Labelling each multi-frame row with a random sibling's gold scores **46.68**, far below the model's 52.33 on the same 664 rows ([error_analysis.log](../logs/r55/explore4/error_analysis.log)). Either annotation is noisy at grade level or grades really change between frames; in both cases most of the +9.32 "perfect grading" headroom cannot be reached. About 10–15 % of each graded defect's errors are confident (probability > 0.8). Of 86 confident errors on multi-frame videos, 45 (52 %) have a sibling whose gold equals the model's prediction, against 44.5 % for all errors there. That small set is a cheap audit list for the owner. Errors cluster within videos (1.5–3.4× sibling error correlation), but 2,826 of 3,136 videos have one frame, so "hard-video mining" reduces to per-image reweighting.

## One decoder change is the only measured engineering gain

Decoder55 is an argmax over log-probabilities plus 21 per-(dimension, grade) biases, a P0 floor and a global clean gate, all fitted by coordinate ascent. The literature warns that this kind of F-measure threshold tuning overfits ([Fan & Lin](https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/~cjlin/papers/thresholding/smooth_acm.pdf)). Here its in-sample optimism is 0.56 points (51.84 in-sample vs 51.28 nested). Plug-in theory says the metric-optimal rule thresholds calibrated probabilities at a metric-dependent value, for F1 at F*/2 ([Lipton et al. 2014](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.1892); [Koyejo et al. 2014](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2014/hash/98053046e0dce5c7d946c67b96a85e18-Abstract.html)). Two rules built on this idea beat the shipped decoder on every nesting partition tested ([robust.log](../logs/r55/explore4/decoding/robust.log); [eu_lambda.log](../logs/r55/explore4/decoding/eu_lambda.log)).

| Decoder (fallback pool) | Δ all rows, mean of 4 partitions | Δ first batch, mean of 4 partitions | Robustness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expected-utility (EU) plug-in, fitted clean scale λ | **+0.52** | +0.29 | All-rows CI90 > 0 on 4/4 partitions; first-batch CI90 crosses 0 on 2/4 |
| Per-cell vote of shipped / ordinal / EU | +0.26 | **+0.28** | P(Δ>0) ≥ 0.93 in all 8 cells; CI90 lower bound > 0 in 7/8 |
| Ordinal cumulative thresholds | +0.26 | +0.25 | Did not replicate on the primary pools (+0.10, −0.15) |

The EU rule computes, for each candidate grade, the first-order marginal gain in Doc2_Strict. It uses fixed training-fold statistics, predicts the grade with the largest positive gain, and sets a row fully clean when its summed defect gain falls below λ·∏P(无问题). Its gain comes almost entirely from the clean term (19.96 vs 19.61, about nine fewer clean images flagged). It is calibration-sensitive: on member p3 alone it *loses* 0.54. Both rules decide per image, fit only on training folds, and comply with AGENTS.md rule 2. The "transductive" EU variant, which re-estimates statistics from the decoded set's own probabilities, uses test-distribution information. It is forbidden and also gained nothing.

**This is the top short-term action, behind a verification gate.** The EU+λ and vote results have **not** yet been reproduced on the exact pool that ships with per-fold λ refitted and logged. Only λ = 1 EU and ordinal were tested on the primary pools, and EU+λ / vote were never run there across partitions. The gate:

- Run `dec_robust.py` / `dec_eu_lambda.py` on the exact shipping pool (fallback as deployed, including TTA settings) across at least four nesting partitions, with λ refitted inside each fold and recorded.
- Ship the vote if its first-batch CI90 lower bound stays > 0 on most partitions. Ship EU+λ only if it also beats the vote on first-batch rows. Otherwise keep Decoder55.
- Check that the chosen λ is stable across folds. A λ that jumps between grid ends signals overfitting.

Expect at most +0.3 on a first-batch-like final set. The bootstrap above is over 1,902 first-batch rows, and a 654-image final set has about 1.7× that SD, so this gain will not be visible in a single reading. Nothing else in post-processing survives: calibrate-then-pool, geometric pooling and rank averaging are significantly *worse* on first-batch rows (−0.46, −0.48, −0.83). Pooled temperature, NLL-learned weights, Saerens prior shift, first-batch-only decoders, pairwise exclusivity constraints and a learned clean gate all fall within −0.17 to +0.09 ([pool.log](../logs/r55/explore4/decoding/pool.log); [decoders.log](../logs/r55/explore4/decoding/decoders.log)). The arithmetic family-balanced pool already has top-label ECE 0.009, consistent with pool-then-calibrate theory ([Rahaman & Thiery 2021](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/file/a70dc40477bc2adceef4d2c90f47eb82-Paper.pdf)).

## Freed VRAM buys diversity, but only orthogonal domains are still open

The ~9 GB freed by dropping the 4-bit VLM (24 − 14.8 GB peak) fits any candidate except InternViT-6B (~12 GB bf16) or a bf16 VLM (~16–17.5 GB). The VLM was redundant in the pool anyway: dropping it costs −0.11 (CI90 [−0.37, +0.15]). The **binding constraint is latency, which has never been measured on a 4090**. The fallback's 3.7 s mean / 6.7 s max was measured on a shared H200 ([fallback_bench_half.json](../results/r55/final/fallback_bench_half.json)). The statement's limit is 15 s per image end to end. The cost tier in statement v2 depends on headroom and throughput under load, not on quantisation itself ([statement v2](../documents/【2026乘风挑战赛】命题18：晴羽观感体系·画质BadCase的无参检测模型%20(2).md)). **Measure the fallback on a real 4090 before adding any member.**

The diversity evidence sets the bar. In the literature, models that differ in *training methodology or data* make the least correlated errors ([Gontijo-Lopes et al., ICLR 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.12899)). In this project, the only modelling step that ever helped was a new family (Phoenix: 51.04 → 51.45). Yet 61 % of the pool's cell errors are shared by all three families, and every family has the same 过度生成 adjacent-grade AUC (0.70–0.75). A greedy composition over 28 models with complete OOF found at most about +0.1. Every frozen "new evidence" bank was null ([REASSESSMENT §2, §6, §8](../docs/REASSESSMENT_AND_PLAN_2026-11-12.md)). Convolutional (ConvNeXt-V2 FCMAE), DINO, EVA, PE-G, RADIO and Haar-wavelet inputs are already inside V16 and p3. What remains untried as a *fine-tuned* member is a different input domain:

| Candidate (dimensions 2 and 3) | Expected Δ (speculative) | VRAM | Est. 4090 latency | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forensic high-pass stream on native pixels (NPR / SRM / DWT residuals, crop-not-resize, DCT top/bottom-k patches), small CNN, targeting 过度生成 / 过锐 | 0 to +0.3 | < 1.5 GB | 0.05–0.3 s | Low cost; may duplicate p3 wavelet maps |
| Restoration-pretrained encoder (NAFNet / Restormer / SwinIR, DA-CLIP controller) fine-tuned on 1:1 tiles | 0 to +0.3 | 1–3 GB | 0.2–0.8 s | Medium; little semantics for anatomy / text |
| Whole-frame dense-tile MIL (gated ABMIL + top-k) on a *different* trunk, for small local 过度生成 inventions | 0 to +0.2 | 1–2.7 GB | 0.3–0.6 s | Same-trunk variants add ≈0 |
| MAE ViT-L/H or Swin-V2-L tower | 0 to +0.15 | 0.5–2 GB | 0.2–0.9 s | FCMAE / DINOv3 analogues were harmful |

Sources: [NPR, CVPR 2024](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Tan_Rethinking_the_Up-Sampling_Operations_in_CNN-based_Generative_Network_for_Generalizable_CVPR_2024_paper.html), [SAFE](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.06741v2), [AIDE](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.19435v2), [FAST-VQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.02595). These forensic methods were validated on fully generated images, not on restoration-induced over-generation. The frozen FLUX-autoencoder and SR-idempotence nulls suggest the generative-trace signal here is weak. Treat this row as the best of a weak set; the estimates are bounded above by the one measured +0.41.

**Screening rule.** Train 2 of 8 folds against a 3-seed control. Proceed to all 8 folds only if pooled ΔRPS ≤ −0.0003 with CI90 excluding 0. The test is pooled decorrelation, not single-tower accuracy: InternViT-6B was the best frozen probe and added ≈0. Further cross-member fusion (cross-view attention across members, stacking, cascades) is closed. Every learned fusion layer at this label count lost to averaging (multinomial stacking −0.11 to −1.12; boosting worse on all six defects). V16 and p3 already contain within-model cross-view contrast residuals. A detection-to-grading cascade is in effect what the decoder already does.

## Data and losses are saturated unless the owner adds frames or labels

**Training objectives (dimension 4) are already aligned with the metric.** `QingyuUnimodalCompositeLoss` already combines soft ordinal Gaussian targets, squared-EMD/RPS (λ = 0.45), an adjacent-grade Thurstone ranking loss with cross-batch memory, an all-clean BCE term and rarity weights of 1.65–1.90, plus last-epoch weight averaging (`code/qingyu/unimodal_xbm_losses.py`, ~L448–498). The literature gains for CORN/CORAL and exact EMD are measured against plain cross-entropy ([CORN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.08851v5); [Hou et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.05916v1)), not against this composite. sigmoidF1-style surrogates mostly buy implicit thresholding ([Bénédict et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10566v3)), which the decoder already does exactly. Noise-robust losses gain little on real, instance-dependent human noise ([CIFAR-N](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.12088)). In the project, the closest noise-handling recipe, cross-fitted soft labels (`xd`), improved one tower's RPS by 8 % and vanished in the pool. Three-seed bagging of a member moved pool RPS by only −0.09 %, more averaging than EMA/SWA would add. Expected pooled gain of any loss swap: below 0.1–0.2. Cross-fitted pool-to-single-student distillation is the one untested loss-side item. It is a *cost* lever: expect −0.3 to −1.0 objective against the ensemble ([Stanton et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.05945)) in exchange for a 1–2 GB engine.

**Data and distribution alignment (dimension 1), engineering side.** Synthetic data transfers only when the simulated process matches the real one. 花屏 decoder corruption with matched clean twins passed (held-out family AUC 0.885 → 0.9999) and is kept for robustness only. Recipes for the graded, content-dependent defects all failed: the FLUX 过度生成 ladder −0.40, ranking ladders −0.72 / −0.90, GAN-restoration fidelity n.s., segmentation bokeh null ([SYNTHLAB](../docs/SYNTHLAB.md); [LEDGER](../results/next55/LEDGER.md)). The literature agrees: global synthetic distortions mismatch non-uniform authentic ones ([Opinion-free BIQA](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2106.14076)). The one untested recipe, local diffusion inpainting for 过度生成 P0 small inventions, targets 12 frames and is worth ≲0.1–0.2 at high fidelity risk. Sample weighting is closed. The first-batch-only and both-batch endpoints are equal after ensembling (+0.12, CI90 [−0.23, +0.54]), and in this separable regime (training loss 1.00 → 0.06 by epoch 8) importance weights fade ([Byrd & Lipton, ICML 2019](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/byrd19a.html)). Hard-example and focal emphasis would amplify the very grade-boundary noise documented above, and the `boost` up-weighting was rejected. Curricula help mainly under noisy labels ([Wu et al., ICLR 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.03107)). One small-loss down-weighting screen (one pe tower, 3 seeds, ~3 GPU-hours) is optional, with an expected pooled gain below 0.1.

**Owner-only levers, separated from what engineering can do alone.** Each of the following needs an owner decision or owner-held data. None may use test or practice images, and no label may be edited without explicit owner approval.

| Owner lever | Evidence | Expected pooled gain | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Export unlabelled frames from first-batch training videos for cross-fitted soft-label self-training (CReST-style resampling of rare positives) | `ssl` recovered 25–32 % of label value on one tower; KVQ / other-source pools null ([REVIEW §9](../docs/REVIEW_AND_ROADMAP_2026-11-11.md); [CReST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.09559)) | +0.1 to +0.4 (extrapolated, not measured in the pool) | Blocked: owner has said no more data |
| Audit the ~86 confident errors on multi-frame videos, especially the 45 where a sibling's gold equals the prediction; the owner decides whether to relabel, down-weight or keep | 52 % sibling agreement vs 44.5 % base ([error_analysis.log](../logs/r55/explore4/error_analysis.log)) | Unquantified; small (≤ 86 cells) | Cheap; owner approval mandatory |
| New same-source labels for 过度生成 / 虚化突兀 | Learning curve +0.068 / +0.076 F1 per doubling vs +0.003–0.017 for saturated defects; ≈+0.8 points per doubling of rare positives ([REASSESSMENT §2.4](../docs/REASSESSMENT_AND_PLAN_2026-11-12.md)) | ≈+0.8 per doubling | The only lever with multi-point evidence |

**Is +1.5 to +2.0 supported?** No. The only measured item gives about +0.3 on first-batch-like rows. The best speculative engineering item is bounded at about +0.3, and both owner data levers together are optimistically around +1.0. A first-batch total of 53.6–54.1 would also exceed the "saturated-defect" ceiling analysis (about 54.4 only if every graded defect matched 纹理损失's F1) by too thin a margin to be credible. First-batch draws with current models give 52.25 ± 0.56 and P(≥54) = 0.2 % ([REASSESSMENT §11.2](../docs/REASSESSMENT_AND_PLAN_2026-11-12.md)). Under a normal approximation with that SD, the probability of a single 654-image reading ≥ 53 is about 6 % at a 52.1 mean, 14 % at 52.4 (decoder shipped) and 24 % at 52.6 (decoder plus a successful new member). These are my calculations, not measurements.

## What not to do: closed levers and their evidence

These levers are closed on measured evidence and should not be reopened without new data:

- **Synthetic graded defects.** 过度生成 FLUX ladder −0.40; ranking ladders −0.72 / −0.90; `ogsr1` n.s.; `bkseg1` 虚化突兀 null; benign-invariance recipes harmful on unseen pipelines. Synthetic data is robustness-only, via 花屏 ([SYNTHLAB](../docs/SYNTHLAB.md)).
- **Batch or domain weighting, first-batch-only decoders, prior shift, and group up-weighting.** Results: +0.12 n.s., −0.11, −0.15, and `boost` grade AUC −0.0040 ([decoders.log](../logs/r55/explore4/decoding/decoders.log); [REASSESSMENT §11](../docs/REASSESSMENT_AND_PLAN_2026-11-12.md)).
- **Calibrate-then-pool, geometric or rank pooling, learned stacking or boosting over members, and pairwise exclusivity constraints.** Results: −0.46, −0.48, −0.83, −0.11 to −1.12, and −0.17 on first-batch rows ([pool.log](../logs/r55/explore4/decoding/pool.log)).
- **Other-source self-training (KVQ and similar).** RPS −0.0002 n.s., and harmful on 花屏 (0.138 → 0.161) ([LEDGER](../results/next55/LEDGER.md)).
- **InternViT-6B, PE-Core-G, DINOv3, another VLM, and frozen IQA / FLUX-AE / SR-idempotence banks.** Results: +0.08 / −0.42; clean collapse; harmful; −0.11 n.s. when dropped; mean ΔRPS +0.0001 to +0.0016 ([REASSESSMENT §6, §9, §11](../docs/REASSESSMENT_AND_PLAN_2026-11-12.md)).
- **EMA/SWA and extra seeds.** Pool RPS −0.09 %.
- **Compliance limits, which are not levers at all.** Transductive EU statistics, EM prior estimation on the test set, and pseudo-labelling test or practice frames all use test-distribution information (AGENTS.md rule 2). Anchoring or overriding with any legacy prediction JSON is forbidden (rule 1).

## Conclusion

The plateau is better understood as a measurement ceiling than as a modelling gap. The fallback already grades as well as two frames of the same video agree with each other. Its largest single error, 过度生成 P1 called P0, sits on the axis where siblings agree only half the time. The engineering side can still bank about +0.3 by replacing 21 searched thresholds with a metric-derived rule, once that rule is re-verified on the shipping pool. It can also afford one decorrelation screen, since the freed VRAM is not the constraint. Beyond that, points have to come from more in-domain images and cleaner rare-grade labels. Both depend on the owner.

The strategic implication is to stop treating 53 as an engineering target and to plan around a centre of about 52.3–52.6 on first-batch-like data, with an SD of 0.56 per reading. With the objective score near saturation, the remaining controllable gains are in the 35 subjective points: a measured 4090 latency, comfortable VRAM headroom and a stable, unquantised engine. Those are worth more than any lever listed above.
